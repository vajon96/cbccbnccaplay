Cadet Enrollment 2026

Your journey toward discipline, leadership, and national service starts here. Step into the shoes of a leader with the Army Wing.

Become a Cadet 🎯

Join an elite group of students dedicated to excellence and patriotism.

  • Step 1: Check Eligibility — Open to students of CBCC with high academic standing and physical fitness.
  • Step 2: Submit Application — Complete the digital form via the link below or visit the Platoon Office.
  • Step 3: Physical & Oral Test — Attend the scheduled recruitment session for fitness and aptitude evaluation.
  • Step 4: Final Selection — Successful candidates will be formally inducted into the BNCC Army Wing.
