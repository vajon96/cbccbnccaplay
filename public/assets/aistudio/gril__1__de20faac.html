Welcome to BNCC

The Bangladesh National Cadet Corps (BNCC) is a tri-services organization, comprising the Army, Navy and Air Force. Its aim is to foster spirit of discipline, leadership, and patriotism among the youth. The Cox's Bazar City College BNCC Platoon (Army Wing) is committed to developing well-rounded individuals who can contribute positively to society.

Our cadets participate in various activities including drills, shooting practice, adventure training, social service, and leadership development programs. We believe in nurturing the potential of every cadet to become responsible citizens and future leaders of our nation.
