CERTIFICATE OF APPRECIATION

This is to certify that Cadet Sajon Dey, Cadet No: 23154352, has demonstrated outstanding dedication, discipline, and leadership as a member of the Bangladesh National Cadet Corps, Army Wing, Cox's Bazar City College BNCC Platoon.

Throughout his cadetship, he has consistently shown exemplary performance in regular drills, parades, and military training sessions. His commitment to excellence and attention to detail have set a high standard for fellow cadets. He has actively represented the platoon in various voluntary and official programs at both upazila and district levels, upholding the core values of the BNCC with honor and integrity.

Cadet Sajon has particularly distinguished himself through his:

  • Exceptional discipline and punctuality
  • Strong leadership qualities during field exercises
  • Willingness to mentor junior cadets
  • Active participation in community service initiatives

In recognition of his sincere commitment, strong sense of responsibility, and commendable leadership potential, Cadet Sajon Dey was officially nominated for the prestigious position of Cadet Sergeant.

As the Platoon Commander, I take pride in acknowledging his contributions to the platoon and express my confidence in his continued success. I firmly believe he will carry forward the ideals of the BNCC and serve the nation with integrity and dedication in all his future endeavors.

Platoon Commander
