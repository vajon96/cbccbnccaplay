A Message from the Platoon

The BNCC is a paramount youth organization dedicated to imparting military training to students to foster discipline, patriotism, and a sense of responsibility towards the nation.

As the Platoon of Cox's Bazar City College, we actively participate in a variety of nation-building and social welfare activities. Our cadets receive rigorous training in military drills, leadership, disaster management, and first aid, preparing them to be responsible citizens and effective leaders in any field of life. We are committed to upholding the motto: **"Knowledge, Discipline, and Service."**

Our Core Values

Knowledge

Fostering continuous learning and military awareness.

Discipline

Instilling a high sense of self-control and adherence to command.

Service
