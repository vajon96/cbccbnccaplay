Welcome to BNCC

Cox's Bazar City College BNCC Platoon (Army Wing) is dedicated to discipline, leadership, and excellence. Through rigorous training and activities, we aim to build confident, capable, and patriotic individuals ready to serve the nation. Join us as we strive for personal growth, teamwork, and the values that shape the future leaders of tomorrow. Together, we uphold the honor and traditions of BNCC while making a meaningful impact on our community and beyond. Discipline, Dedication, Determination – This is our motto.
